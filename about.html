Our story

Built by curlfriends.

For decades, Indian women with curls had two options: hide them or fight them. We built a third option.

The why

India's first curl-specific brand.

The Curl Co was born from frustration. The founders — Indian women with curls — were tired of products designed for straight hair, of salons that defaulted to straightening, and of a culture that whispered curls weren't "professional".

So we built the brand we wished existed: sulphate-free, silicone-free, paraben-free products formulated for Indian humidity, water hardness, and the unique density of South Asian curls. Every formula goes through curlfriends — not lab subjects — for real-world testing.

Today over 50,000 curlfriends use our routines. From 2A waves in Bangalore to 4A coils in Cochin, we're rewriting what curly hair care looks like in India.
