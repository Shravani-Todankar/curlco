How to Use Your Curl Co. Products

Your best wash day is one tutorial away.

The Routine

Your Simple Curl Routine

Three steps. One wash day. Curls that last.

01

Cleanse

Wet hair thoroughly. Massage Curl Shampoo into the scalp in small circles — never pile or scrub the lengths. Let the foam slip through your curls as you rinse. Rinse with cool water to keep the cuticle smooth and shine intact. Repeat only if needed (heavy buildup or after a workout).

02

Nourish

While hair is still soaking wet, apply Curl Conditioner from mid-lengths to ends. Detangle with fingers or a wide-tooth comb under the shower spray — never on dry hair. Leave on for 3–5 minutes, then rinse with cool water (leave a little in if your curls are extra thirsty).

03

Style

On dripping-wet hair, smooth a generous palmful of Curl Cream or Curl Gel through the lengths using praying-hands and scrunching motions. For extra shine, finish with two drops of Frizz Control Serum. Plop in a microfibre towel for 15 minutes, then air-dry or diffuse on low.
