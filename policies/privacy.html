Privacy policy.

What information do we collect?

When you place an order or sign up for our newsletter we collect your name, email, phone, shipping address and payment information. Payment data is processed by Razorpay and not stored on our servers.

How do we use your information?

  • To process and ship your orders
  • To send order updates and shipping notifications
  • To send marketing emails (only if you opt in)
  • To improve our products and service

Cookies

We use cookies to remember your cart, wishlist and preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser but parts of the site may not work as expected.

Third parties

We share data only with the partners required to fulfil your order (logistics, payment, email). We never sell your personal data.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing contact@curlco.in.
