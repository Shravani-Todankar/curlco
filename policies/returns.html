Returns & Refunds

Last updated · January 2026

Damaged or defective products

We apologize if your products arrived damaged. Please write to us at contact@curlco.in within 48 hours of receipt with photos of the damaged product and the original packaging. We'll send a replacement as soon as possible.

Returns on opened products

For hygiene and safety reasons, we cannot accept returns of opened or used products unless they are defective.

Refund timeline

Once a return is approved, refunds are processed within 7 business days to your original method of payment.

Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, cancellations are no longer possible. Contact us as quickly as possible if you need to cancel.
