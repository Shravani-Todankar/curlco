Shipping Policy

Last updated · January 2026

When and how will my order ship?

We ship from Mumbai, India. Orders are dispatched within 5 working days of payment confirmation. Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email.

Delivery time

Standard delivery across India takes 3-7 business days from dispatch, depending on your PIN code. Metro cities typically receive within 3-4 business days.

Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on all prepaid orders above ₹599
  • Orders under ₹599: flat ₹49 shipping
  • COD orders: an additional ₹49 COD handling fee may apply

Order tracking

If you haven't received your order within 10 business days of receiving your shipping confirmation email, please contact us at contact@curlco.in or +91 90826 43562 with your name and order number.

Address accuracy

Kindly ensure that the shipping address provided is accurate. We cannot refund or replace orders that fail to deliver due to incorrect addresses.

International shipping

Currently we ship only within India. We're working on bringing The Curl Co worldwide soon.
